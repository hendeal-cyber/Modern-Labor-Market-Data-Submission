Energy Companies
List of Suppliers
These energy service companies have met the eligibility requirements established by the New York State Public Service Commission and indicated their intention to serve our NY customers. They don't work for or represent O&R.
If you’re contacted by these energy service companies, remember to exercise smart shopping sense:
There's no need to show your O&R bill or reveal your account number unless you decide to enroll with that energy service company
Remember: It's your energy, your choice. You're under no obligation to switch or choose an energy service company.
For service in New York, we encourage you to shop around through our eBids program (forthcoming), or by contacting individual energy service companies for service information and pricing options.
If you choose an energy service company for your electricity or natural gas, they’ll ask you for your O&R account number to enroll. By providing your account number, you’re authorizing O&R to switch you to your supplier of choice.
What happens next?
After you give your 10-digit O&R account number to the energy service company, they’ll work with O&R to coordinate your energy needs.
You’ll get a letter from O&R confirming your switch to a new energy service company with the service start date. You’ll have five days to
contact O&R
if you change your mind. O&R charges no fees to switch suppliers.

The energy service companies (ESCOs) listed above have met the eligibility requirements of the New York State Public Service Commission (PSC) and are actively marketing in New York. They have indicated that they will supply electricity and/or natural gas to residential and small commercial customers. These companies may decide to change their services without notifying us. To be sure you have the most recent information, click on the links above to visit the energy service companies’ websites. Or call the energy service company for additional information.
The PSC publishes the ESCO Residential Complaint Rate Scorecard (
www.dps.ny.gov
), which reflects complaint rates for ESCOs that serve at least 1,000 customers in New York State. The rates are based on complaints received, whether or not the ESCO was determined to be at fault or adequately resolved the consumer's complaint. To file a complaint with the PSC, visit
www.dps.ny.gov/complaints
.
A Word About Prices
Energy is a commodity that is bought and sold, so prices can fluctuate. To find out about an individual energy service company’s prices, please check their website or give them a call.
